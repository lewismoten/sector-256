# ANT

Langton's Ant on a centered, wrapping 256×200 high-resolution Commodore 64
field. The ant begins in the center of a blank grid. On a white cell it turns
right; on a black cell it turns left. It flips the pixel's color and moves
forward after every turn.

The program advances sixteen ant steps per video frame so the early symmetric
patterns and eventual highway develop at a useful speed. The much larger field
gives the highway room to escape the initial pattern. Press SPACE to reset the
field. RUN/STOP returns to the LAB category.
