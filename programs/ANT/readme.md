# ANT

Langton's Ant on a wrapping 40×25 Commodore 64 field. The ant begins in the
center of a blank grid. On a white cell it turns right; on a black cell it turns
left. It flips the cell's color and moves forward after every turn.

The program advances eight ant steps per video frame so the early symmetric
patterns and eventual highway develop at a useful speed. Press SPACE to reset
the field. RUN/STOP returns to the LAB category.
