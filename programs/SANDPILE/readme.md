# SANDPILE

An Abelian sandpile growing on a 38×23 Commodore 64 field. Sixteen grains are
dropped at the center each video frame. Whenever a cell reaches four grains it
topples, sending one grain to each orthogonal neighbor. The black border absorbs
grains that leave the field.

Stable heights zero through three appear as black, white, red, and cyan.
Their repeated topplings form the characteristic sandpile fractal. Press SPACE
to clear and restart; RUN/STOP returns to the DEMOS category.
